How the process works

Three stages, from planning to delivery.

  1. Before you request

    • Confirm that funding is available.
    • Gather the quote, vendor details, and account information.
    • Check whether a supported standard or existing agreement meets the need.

  2. IT review

    • Hardware and software purchases are reviewed for compatibility and support.
    • Vendors handling University data may require a security and risk assessment.
    • Include the business purpose and any technical requirements.

  3. Submit and track

    • Send the completed requisition with supporting documentation.
    • Allow time for budget, IT, procurement, and contract review when applicable.
    • For help along the way, contact IT Purchasing.

A smoother purchasing process

Complete information at the start helps reviewers route your request correctly and reduces follow-up. Depending on the item, vendor, and agreement, a purchase may involve budget confirmation, technology review, procurement approval, and legal review.

Tip: Attach the current quote and note whether the request is a new purchase, renewal, or replacement.
